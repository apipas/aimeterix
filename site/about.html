About

AI Meterix is an open-source project by Apipas s.r.o., a software company in Prague, Czechia.

🎯

Why we built it

AI has a real cost in tokens and in electricity, and it is hard to see. We wanted one honest number per day, measured on our own machines, without sending anything to a cloud service.
