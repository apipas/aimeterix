AI Meterix @VERSION@

Measures what AI costs on this Mac: the energy the chip uses, the tokens each model reads and writes, local or in the cloud, and what both cost in your currency.

Default settings

The first aimeterix start uses these. To change any of them first, run aimeterix init.

Proxyhttp://127.0.0.1:8787, where your apps send their AI calls
Dashboardhttp://127.0.0.1:8788, accounts user and admin
Local model servernone; set it to your llama.cpp or Ollama address
DatabaseSQLite in ~/Library/Application Support/aimeterix
Samplesevery 10 seconds; raw data kept 30 days, hourly totals 365

Everything listens on this Mac only. Prompts, answers and API keys are never stored.

What gets installed

The program and its own Python in /usr/local/ai-meterix, and the aimeterix command in /usr/local/bin. Nothing starts by itself. Your password is needed once, to install; AI Meterix never runs as root.
